**Subject:** Poster offer for ConCon: perturbation beats interrogation when
testing consciousness-theory properties in AI systems

Hello,

I have registered interest for ConCon and want to offer a poster for the poster
session, if there is a process and space for it.

The work: two preregistered projects that implement consciousness theories
rather than argue about them. The first built an executable test of global
workspace theory's capacity claim on language-model agents; the headline is that
the claim's value depends on assumptions the theory never states, and behavioral
indicator tests can be passed by prompted imposters. The second found the
constructive alternative: perturbation. Turn the architecture's own capacity
knob and compare self-reports to ground truth, and the real system separates
from imposters at roughly a quarter of the trials interrogation needs; the
method fails in a measurable way when the knob value leaks. The origin of the
method is a companion project implementing the psychedelic theories REBUS and
ALBUS, where dose as intervention and report as readout is the native paradigm.

Everything is public, preregistered before code, with failed predictions
reported: github.com/ArtHangup/rebus-albus-sim (and the gwbench repository
alongside). Happy to send either paper. If posters are invitation-only or full,
no problem; I will attend regardless.
