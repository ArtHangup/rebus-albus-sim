# Journal options for papers 1 and 2

Compiled 2026-08-12 from publisher pages and journal databases. Fees change;
verify on the journal page at submission time. Context: independent author, no
institutional APC fund, openness already achieved via the public repo and the
Zenodo DOI, so paying for gold open access buys audience and prestige only, not
openness. That flips the default toward no-fee hybrid venues with green
self-archiving.

## The shortlist

| Venue | Cost to publish | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neuroscience of Consciousness (Oxford) | APC about $2,156, full OA, no free route | Best room for paper 1: published ALBUS itself; theory-friendly; IF 3.8 | The right readers; the only argument against is the fee |
| Computational Brain and Behavior (Springer, Soc. for Mathematical Psychology) | $0 via the subscription route (OA optional at $3,390) | Strong for paper 1: modeling-literate reviewers who value preregistration | Free path recommended; preprint stays up (green OA) |
| Journal of Psychopharmacology (SAGE, BAP) | $0: no submission or publication fees, hybrid | Strong for paper 2: the clinical psychedelics audience; Carhart-Harris's home venue | Risk: desk rejection as simulation-only; the exposure framing is the pitch |
| Collabra: Psychology (UC Press) | APC $975; waiver fund, request BEFORE acceptance | Good general-psychology home; transparency-first editorial culture rewards prereg | Cheapest gold OA with a real waiver path |
| Psychology of Consciousness (APA) | No APC found (standard APA subscription model) | Moderate: consciousness scope, lower profile | Solid backup for paper 1 |
| Journal of Psychedelic Studies (Akademiai) | APC 1,500 euro (was 990 before May 2026) | On-topic, friendlier bar, modest prestige | Backup for paper 2 |
| PLOS ONE | APC about $2,000; case-by-case waivers | Soundness-only review suits a transparent paper | High pass odds, low audience value |
| Frontiers in Psychiatry | APC about $3,000 | Where Wolff and Zeifman published; high acceptance | Costly; mixed prestige signal |
| Peer Community In (PCI) | $0 | Community peer review of preprints; a formal reviewed object at zero cost | Unconventional but real; PCI-endorsed preprints are citable as peer reviewed |

## Recommended strategy

1. **Paper 1 first, one venue at a time.** Primary: Neuroscience of Consciousness
   if the roughly $2,200 fee is acceptable; it is the room where the REBUS and
   ALBUS debate actually lives, and an acceptance there lands with exactly the
   people the poster will meet. Free alternative with nearly equal review
   quality: Computational Brain and Behavior via the subscription route, where
   reviewers are modeling specialists who will treat the preregistration
   discipline as a feature.
2. **Paper 2 to the Journal of Psychopharmacology** once paper 1 is decided:
   zero cost, the clinical audience the exposure framing was built for, and the
   BAP readership includes the psychedelic therapy trialists whose designs the
   discriminating predictions target.
3. **Whatever happens, the credential wall falls with the first acceptance.**
   That also reopens PsyArXiv for everything after.
4. Journal requirements to expect in revision: IMRaD restructure (retitle the
   claims-scoping section to Discussion), deeper engagement with the reviewer's
   preferred literature, possibly importing a robustness arm from the paper 2
   set, and softened rhetoric in a few flagged spots. None of these threaten the
   results.
5. Suggest methods-oriented reviewers where the form allows; do not suggest
   principals of either theory (conflict), and expect one of them anyway.
